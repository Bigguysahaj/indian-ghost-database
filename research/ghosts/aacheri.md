# Aacheri

## Sources

## Region

Kumaon and the southern Himalayas.

## Behaviours

Reported tradition, scoped to the cited account:

- A female hill spirit, described as a young girl in some accounts and as women in others.
- Stays on mountain summits by day.
- Descends at dusk; her shadow is said to bring illness.
- Children are especially vulnerable in the stories.
- Some accounts connect her with a violent death.

## Abilities

`creative_interpretation`

- Sickening shadow (from `behavior`): contact with her shadow makes a character ill.
- Preys on the young (from `prey`): she seeks out child characters first.

## Weaknesses

`creative_interpretation`

- Scarlet thread (from `protections`): a character wearing it is shielded from her illness. The source reports a folk custom, not medical protection, and does not say that other red items or clothing repel her.

## Triggers

`creative_interpretation`

- Dusk (from `behavior`): the encounter starts as she comes down at dusk.
- Summits by day (from `habitat`): in daylight, her presence is limited to mountain summits.
